The Brain Loop: why the thought keeps coming back

Researchers who scan the brains of people who ruminate find a pattern across three large networks. One turns attention inward to the self. One turns it outward to a task. One works like a switch between them. In a rumination loop the switch sticks on the inward side. Explore the loop below, then flip to engagement and try the three moves that help it let go.
