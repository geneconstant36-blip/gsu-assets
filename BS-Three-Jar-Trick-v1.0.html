The Three Jar Trick

The simple system that changes everything.

Money can feel complicated, but ten-year-old Marcus showed us it's actually simple. Instead of spending everything the moment it arrives, or saving all of it and feeling miserable, you split it three ways. Type in what you got and watch the jars fill.
