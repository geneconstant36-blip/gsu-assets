The Concrete Shift: from “Why?” to “How?”

Rumination runs on abstract “why” questions: Why am I like this? Why does this always happen? Helpful thinking runs on concrete ones: What exactly happened? What is one step I can take? Walk the five steps below, watch where autopilot leads, then practice the shift until it comes on its own.
