The Wishing Lamp

Three wishes. One honest genie. Every road he gives you is free.
“Ahh — free at last, and glad of it! I am GENO the Genie, keeper of this old, old lamp. You have rubbed it — so I owe you three wishes. But I am an honest genie: I will not lie to you, and I will not do your growing for you. What I give is better than a trick — the true road to your wish, and every road I give is free. So tell me… what do you wish for?”
