The Wishing Lamp

Three wishes. One honest genie. Every road he gives you is free.
“Ahh — free at last, and glad of it! I am GENO the Genie, keeper of this old, old lamp. You have rubbed it — so I owe you three wishes. But I am an honest genie: I will not lie to you, and I will not do your growing for you. What I give is better — the true road to your wish, and every road I give is free.”
✦ GENO the Genie speaks ✦
Make a wish below, or tap a doorway to try one. I am listening…
🎤 Turn your microphone on before you rub the lamp — the Genie listens and answers aloud. No microphone? You can simply type your wish to him in the chat window that opens.
Speak your wish, and the Genie answers
“The lamp was never the magic. YOU are. Amazing, you are.”

So the Genie turns his last question to you:
Now — what do YOU wish for? And what could you do — together with someone — to make it come true?

Wish it. Then go and build it. Rub the lamp again whenever you like —
for a mind that keeps wishing to learn never runs out of wishes.
